# Optional advanced factors

These ideas were retained as design references from auxiliary skills. They are not permission to use an unverified secondary calculator.

## Safe to derive from canonical data

- chart ruler and full dispositor chain;
- element and modality distributions, with explicit handling of duplicates and angles;
- aspect networks and major configurations when every edge passes the canonical orb rules;
- angularity, sect, major essential dignity/debility, retrogradation, combustion and under-beams;
- Lot of Fortune when the time is known and the day/night formula is recorded.

## Optional and separately gated

- fixed-star contacts require a defined star catalog, epoch, coordinate frame, and tight orb policy;
- additional Arabic lots require an explicit formula and sect reversal rule;
- Chiron, Lilith, asteroids, and minor aspects require the user to request them and the calculator to record their source;
- aspect-pattern labels must be derived from the actual aspect graph, not visual impression.

## Hellenistic method reminders

- Select techniques by the user's question instead of applying every technique.
- Separate a planet's topic, condition, and ability to act.
- Read sect, dignity, angularity, applying/separating, and lunar phase as modifiers, not standalone fate claims.
- Conditions describe how a function operates; they do not supply a biographical event by themselves.

Do not import Vedic divisional charts, Dasha, Yoga, SAV, Shadbala, or sidereal rules into this tropical natal workflow.
